# UIPref.md - User Design Preferences & Directives

- **Theme**: Zelda A Link to the Past Dark World pixel-art aesthetic.
- **Protagonist**: Shadow Link (crimson eyes, dark shadow blade, spin attack).
- **Intro & Menu**: Ominous pixel-art start screen featuring procedural Web Audio dark chiptune synth music.
- **Controls**: WASD / Arrow Keys for movement, J or Z for Sword Slash, K or X for Spin Attack / Magic Dash, Space or E for Interact / Dialogue, Tab for Inventory, M for Map/Telemetry.
- **Palette**: Tri-Color (#000000 black, #FFFFFF white, #DE2626 vivid red accent).
- **HUD**: Hearts bar, Magic meter, Active Weapon/Item slot, Telemetry FPS counter, Sub-tab toolbar.
- **Meta-UI**: 3-Tab About / Meta-UI with Author Attribution to Elton Boehnen, Change Log, and BEJSON 105 Reusable Schema Exporter with Copy/Save/ZIP options.
